Getting Started

Step 1: Create Your Account

  1. Visit grantunion.vercel.app
  2. Click "Sign Up" in the top navigation
  3. Enter your email address and create a strong password
  4. Verify your email via the confirmation link sent to your inbox
  5. Complete your profile with name, phone, and username
Password Requirements: Your password must contain at least 8 characters, including uppercase letters, numbers, and special characters.

Step 2: Complete Your Profile

  1. Log in to your dashboard
  2. Navigate to Profile section
  3. Fill in all required information:
    • Full name
    • Phone number
    • Username
    • Profile picture (optional)
  4. Click "Save Changes"

Step 3: KYC Verification

Know Your Customer (KYC) verification is required to unlock full platform features and higher withdrawal limits.

  1. Go to Dashboard → KYC Verification
  2. Upload the following documents:
    • Valid ID (Passport, Driver's License, or National ID)
    • Proof of address (Utility bill, Bank statement, or Lease agreement)
    • Selfie with your ID
  3. Wait for verification (usually completed within 24 hours)
  4. Receive approval notification via email

Withdrawals

Withdrawal Process

  1. Go to Dashboard → Withdraw
  2. Enter withdrawal amount (minimum $200)
  3. Select payment method (Bitcoin, Ethereum, or Bank Transfer)
  4. Enter payment details (wallet address or bank account)
  5. Submit withdrawal request
  6. Receive admin approval (24-48 hours typical)

Frequently Asked Questions

How often are returns credited?

Daily ROI is credited to your account balance every 24 hours at 12:00 AM UTC. You can view accrued returns in real-time on your dashboard.

Can I withdraw my capital before the plan completes?

No, capital is locked until the plan completion date to ensure the investment fulfills its term. However, you can withdraw accrued ROI at any time.

What if I lose my password?

Use the "Forgot Password" link on the login page to reset it via email verification. Follow the link in your email to create a new password.

Are there any hidden fees?

No hidden fees. All fees are transparent and displayed before you confirm any transaction. Withdrawal fees are minimal.

Can I invest in multiple plans simultaneously?

Yes! You can have multiple active investment plans at the same time. There is no limit to how many concurrent investments you can maintain.

How does the referral bonus work?

You earn 5% commission on each referred member's investment. There's no limit to how many people you can refer or how much you can earn.

What happens if I encounter technical issues?

Contact our support team immediately. They will assist you with any technical problems, account issues, or concerns within 24 hours.
